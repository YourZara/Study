Repeated in the past papers (do these first)

  • Sequence detector (10 marks): 011, 110, 010, 101 with T / SR / D flip-flops. Every paper.
  • Synchronous counter design (mod-6, mod-10, 2-bit up/down) with T, SR, JK.
  • Asynchronous counter (BCD/mod-10, mod-12, 3-bit up/down, 3-bit ripple).
  • Shift registers: SIPO/PISO with timing diagram; Johnson counter.
  • K-map 4 variables (with don't cares) + logic diagram; decoder/MUX realization.
  • Full adder / subtractor using MUX; octal encoder; magnitude comparator.
  • Codes: Gray↔binary, excess-3, 2's complement, BCD addition, hex→octal.
  • Short notes: TTL/CMOS characteristics, CMOS NAND/TTL NOR, frequency counter, time measurement, digital vs analog.
  • Flip-flop modification: SR→JK, D→JK, latch vs flip-flop.

How to score 80%+

  1. All 12 questions are compulsory, so no chapter can be skipped.
  2. Design questions are the main marks (counters, machine, K-map ≈ 35). Learn the steps in order and show the table, equations, circuit and timing diagram.
  3. Draw neat labelled diagrams; state assumptions.
  4. Practise each solved example, then change the numbers.
