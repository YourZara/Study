3. NAND and NOR are universal gates [3]

A gate is universal if NOT, AND and OR (a complete set) can be built from it alone.

NAND as universal gate

  • NOT: (A·A)' = A'
  • AND: ((A·B)')' = A·B (NAND followed by NAND used as NOT)
  • OR: (A'·B')' = A + B (De Morgan)

NOR as universal gate

  • NOT: (A+A)' = A'
  • OR: ((A+B)')' = A+B
  • AND: (A'+B')' = A·B (De Morgan)

Hence any Boolean function can be realized with NAND gates only or NOR gates only. ∎

8. 4-bit SIPO shift register – data 1010 [5]

Working: four D flip-flops (A, B, C, D) share one clock. Serial data enters FF-A; on every clock edge each FF copies the output of the FF before it (QA→DB, QB→DC, QC→DD). After 4 clock pulses all 4 bits are available together at QAQBQCQD (parallel output). Data is applied MSB first (1,0,1,0) at positive-edge clock; initially all FFs are cleared.

Fig: 4-bit SIPO shift register. Parallel outputs QA, QB, QC, QD.

After the 4th pulse QDQCQBQA = 1010 (the first bit entered has travelled to FF-D).

9. Asynchronous BCD (decade) counter [5]

Principle: a 4-bit ripple (asynchronous) counter counts 0000 to 1111. To make it a decade counter it is forced to clear as soon as it tries to reach 1010 (decimal 10). Four JK flip-flops are used with J = K = 1 (toggle mode), negative-edge triggered. The external clock drives FF-Q0; the output of each FF clocks the next one.

A 2-input NAND gate takes Q3 and Q1 (the first state in which both are 1 is 1010) and its output is connected to the active-low asynchronous CLEAR of all FFs. When the count reaches 1010 the NAND output goes low, all FFs are cleared instantly to 0000 and the count restarts. State 1010 exists only for a few nanoseconds (a glitch).

Fig: FF chain, J=K=1, clock of each FF taken from Q of the previous FF; NAND(Q3,Q1) → CLR of all four FFs.

Fig: timing diagram of the decade counter (count 0 to 9, then reset). Q0 toggles on every falling clock edge, Q1 on the falling edge of Q0, Q2 on that of Q1, Q3 on that of Q2. At the 10th falling edge Q3Q1 = 11 → reset → 0000.

The counter divides the clock frequency by 10 (mod-10).

* 1010 appears momentarily and is cleared immediately.

12. Main characteristics of digital logic families [4]

  1. Propagation delay (tpd): average time for a change at the input to appear at the output; measures speed.
  2. Power dissipation: power consumed by a gate (static + dynamic); lower is better.
  3. Speed–power product: tpd × P; figure of merit (pJ) – smaller is better.
  4. Fan-in: maximum number of inputs a gate can have.
  5. Fan-out: maximum number of similar gate inputs a gate output can drive without leaving the valid logic levels.
  6. Noise margin: maximum noise voltage tolerated without wrong output: NMH = VOH(min) − VIH(min), NML = VIL(max) − VOL(max).
  7. Voltage levels: VIH, VIL, VOH, VOL and supply voltage VCC/VDD.
  8. Current parameters: IIH, IIL, IOH, IOL. Others: operating temperature range, cost, and output type (totem-pole, open-collector, tri-state).
