1. Official Syllabus Weightage & Exam Blueprint

According to the official Tribhuvan University Institute of Engineering (IOE) curriculum for ENEE 154 (BEI / BCT), the final theoretical examination carries 60 marks (Pass Mark: 24, Time: 3 hours).

Syllabus Note on Marks Distribution

The official syllabus notes: "There may be minor deviation in marks distribution." In actual past papers (2081 Ashwin, 2082 Baishakh, 2082 Bhadra, 2083 Baishakh), questions 1–4 consistently test Circuit Analysis & Transients (23–24 marks), questions 5–6 test Frequency Response & Two-Port Parameters (13–14 marks), and questions 7–10 test Machines & Electromagnetics (23–24 marks).

2. Recommended Learning Sequence

To score top marks in IOE engineering examinations, do not memorize final answers or problem solutions blindly. Follow this seven-phase sequence:

01
Concept & Physics — Understand the physical behavior of inductors, capacitors, magnetic cores, commutators, and rotating fields.
02
Governing Equations — State the mathematical relations, variable definitions, and boundary conditions clearly.
03
Step-by-Step Derivation — Practice writing derivations algebraically without skipping steps (e.g. series RLC ODE, Laplace s-domain impedance, DC motor torque, RMF constant magnitude \(1.5 \Phi_m\)).
04
Standard Numerical Layout — Apply the 8-part format: Given → Required → Principle → Equation → Substitution → Calculation → Final Answer with Units → Engineering Check.
05
PYQ Cross-Verification — Match every solved problem with past question variations to see how IOE twists parameters.
06
Last-Day Rapid Revision — Review comparison tables, common sign/unit traps, and the final formula sheet.

3. Major Exam Trends Identified Across 2081–2083 Papers

Initial Conditions (\(t=0^+\)) Tested 4/4 Exams

Appears every semester in Question 2 for 5 marks. In fact, the exact same two-mesh circuit with a \(1\text{H}\) inductor, \(10\,\Omega\) resistors, and \(1\text{F}\) capacitor was repeated in 2081 Ashwin and 2083 Baishakh!

Asymptotic Bode Plots Tested 4/4 Exams

Always carries 7 or 8 marks. Features double poles at the origin (\(1/s^2\)), quadratic poles, or left-half plane zeros. Requires systematic corner frequency tables and magnitude/phase slope sketches.

Transformer Core Flux & Tests Tested 4/4 Exams

The proof that "main magnetic flux in transformer core is independent of load" appeared in 2081 Ashwin, 2082 Bhadra, and 2083 Baishakh. OC/SC test parameter calculation appeared in 2082 Baishakh.

DC Motor Starting & Back EMF Tested 4/4 Exams

Conceptual justification of why DC motors draw huge starting current and how a 3-point starter protects the armature, along with speed control numerical calculations (\(N \propto E_b/\Phi\)).
