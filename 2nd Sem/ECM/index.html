What repeats in PQA (study priority)

  • Almost every paper: nodal or mesh with dependent sources + matrix; initial conditions at \(t=0^+\); classical RLC; Laplace switched circuit; asymptotic Bode; two-port Z/ABCD/h; short notes on machines.
  • Machines block: transformer flux / OC–SC / efficiency; DC shunt numerical + back EMF / starter; 3-ph IM slip–torque + why \(N_r < N_s\); stepper / universal / hysteresis notes.
  • High-weight theory: Ch2 (10) and Ch4–Ch5 (8 each) — allocate revision time accordingly.
